4. What a yes means

  • D1
    The pane list
    Ten panes become nine: Island, Island Content, Keyboard & Mouse, Display & Sound, Capture, Clipboard & Files, Downloads, Launcher Extras, Mac Tweaks. Agents, Power and Notices go as panes; nothing they held is lost.
  • D2
    Off means gone
    A switch that is off is one row, everywhere in Settings: Notchcast panes and the stock panes (Clipboard, Snippets, File Search, Window Management, Quicklinks, Calendar, Applications, System Actions, Dictation › Cleanup). Nothing is shown dimmed under a switch that is off.
  • D3
    “Small Commands” becomes “Small Tools”
    Only the name on the switch. Its three commands keep their names, aliases and hotkeys.
  • D4
    Nothing you set is touched
    Every switch keeps its state, every alias and hotkey stays. No setting is renamed or reset.

Three links stop opening a pane, because the panes are gone: notchcast-agents, notchcast-power, notchcast-notices. Nothing in the app uses them.

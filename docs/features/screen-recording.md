# Screen Recording

A Notchcast add-on (`Tinycast/Features/Notchcast/ScreenRecording/`, switch `recording`, Settings › Notchcast › Capture, off by default). **Record Area**, **Record Window** and **Record Screen** record with ScreenCaptureKit, with system audio and optionally the microphone, to H.264 or HEVC in a `.mov` or `.mp4`. While it records, the island shows a stop control with the elapsed time; after stop a trimmer shows the clip with start and end handles and offers Save, Copy or Discard.

## Invariants

- **One recording at a time, decided in `RecordingState`.** Every command and callback goes through it (`Model/RecordingState.swift`): idle → selecting → starting → recording → stopping → trimming → idle. Any Record command while recording stops it, so one hotkey starts and stops; while picking, starting, stopping or trimming a command does nothing. Callbacks for an older recording (a late pick, a late start, a late trimmer answer) carry its operation id and stand down.
- **The island is reached only through `context.presenter`.** Recording sets one `IslandStatus` (id `recording`, `stop.circle`, the elapsed time as its text) whose tap stops, plus one pill-menu row "Stop Recording". The text fits the status's four characters: `m:ss` under ten minutes, then `NNm`, then `HhMM` (`RecordingClock`). Beside a notch that is all that shows; nothing else of the add-on draws on the island. Results are notices with id `recording`: "Saving Recording…" (untitled progress, compact on a notch), then "Recording Saved" (tap reveals the file in Finder), "Recording Copied", or a failure.
- **A recorded clip is never lost by the add-on.** The clip is written to a work folder (`Screen Recordings` in the temporary directory) and moves to its destination only after the export succeeds; a failed export keeps it and says so. Switching the add-on off while recording or trimming stops the capture and saves the clip whole, untrimmed, to the recording folder. Only Discard deletes it. The trimmer has no click-away or Esc close for the same reason.
- **Trimming passes through where it can.** `RecordingExport.plan` keeps an untrimmed clip as recorded (a move), cuts with `AVAssetExportPresetPassthrough` when `AVAssetExportSession.compatibility` allows it for the container, and re-encodes at `AVAssetExportPresetHighestQuality` only otherwise. Handles never cross: a handle dragged past the other gives way, never the clip, which keeps at least `RecordingTrim.minimumLength` (0.2 s). A cut is written to a hidden `.notchcast-partial-` sibling of its destination and moved in only once whole.
- **Capture is a file, not frames.** `ScreenRecordingCapture` adds an `SCRecordingOutput` (codec and container from Settings) to one `SCStream`; the frames its sample outputs receive are dropped. The stream is sized in even pixels rounded down, at least 32 a side; a recorded window scales into the size it started at; shadows are left out; the system pointer is recorded, since nothing draws one afterwards. ScreenCaptureKit's refusal reads as the Screen Recording access failure, a missing display or window as "Nothing to record there." Our own process's audio is excluded. Whether the island shows in a recording follows Island › "Show in screenshots and videos", like Screenshots: no window of ours is excluded by the add-on. A capture that stops by itself (its window closed, sharing stopped from the menu bar) goes to the trimmer with what it recorded.
- **Two surfaces of its own, both outside the island** (design-system.md §12 lets an add-on open none; these are the issue's own): the selection surface, a shielding-level dimming panel per display like Screenshots' (drag an area; or click the highlighted window; Esc or a right click cancels), and the trimmer, upstream's floating-panel recipe (`CameraPanel`/`CameraView`: `panelScrim` over `GlassEffectView`, `Theme.Radius.dialog`, `Theme.Size.cameraStage` for the video, `.modalAction` buttons; Return saves).
- **Permissions are asked from the command.** Screen Recording through `CGRequestScreenCaptureAccess` before the picker opens; Microphone, only with "Record microphone" on, before capture starts. A refusal is a failure notice and records nothing.

## Derived code

The trim sanitising, even pixel sizes, staged export and the stream's configuration and failure mapping are adapted from Vorssaint (`vorssaint/vorssaint-utils@89d2626`, `Services/Recorder/`, GPL-3.0-or-later); each derived file keeps its notice and has its line in `notchcast/licenses/borrowed.md`. Vorssaint writes frames through `AVAssetWriter` for its editor's compositor (drawn pointer, zoom, backgrounds, GIF); this add-on has no compositor, so it keeps `SCRecordingOutput` and AVFoundation's own export.

## Settings

Under the switch: Record system audio (on), Record microphone (off), Video codec (H.264, HEVC), File format (MOV, MP4), Recording folder (Desktop until chosen). Stored in the `.notchcast` suite under `notchcast.recording.*`; none is carried by `settings.json`. Files are named as macOS names its own, `Screen Recording <yyyy-MM-dd> at <HH.mm.ss>.<ext>`, counting up on a clash. Copy writes the trimmed file into the work folder and puts its file URL on the clipboard.

## Proof

- `Tests/nc-ScreenRecording-model-test.swift`: every state transition, the clock text, trim clamping and the 10 s → 2…8 s → 6 s case, the export plan and its staged name, window picking and area geometry, pixel sizes, options and file names.
- `Tests/nc-ScreenRecording-module-test.swift`: the shipped descriptor and module with a fake system, clock and presenter (no capture, no sleeps): record a window, the status ticking 0:00 → 0:10, stop from the status, the menu row and a second command, the trimmer, Save cut to 2…8 s by passthrough into the Desktop, Copy re-encoding into the clipboard, Discard, name clashes, permission and microphone refusals, a capture ending by itself, an empty recording, a failed export keeping the clip, and switch-off while picking, recording and trimming.

Not verified by a harness: the live ScreenCaptureKit capture, the exported file playing with sound, the selection surface and trimmer on screen.
